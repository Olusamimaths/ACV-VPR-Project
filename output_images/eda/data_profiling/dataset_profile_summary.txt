Dataset Profiling EDA Summary
======================================================================

1. Are the datasets balanced at the split level?
   GardensPoint is balanced at 200 day references and 200 night queries.
   Campus is smaller and less balanced at 50 day references and 64 night queries.

2. Are image sizes consistent?
   GardensPoint day mean size: 960x540
   GardensPoint night mean size: 640x360
   Campus day mean size: 640x480
   Campus night mean size: 640x480
   GardensPoint uses different day and night resolutions, while campus is size-consistent.

3. How different are the day and night image distributions?
   GardensPoint brightness means: day 97.1, night 127.6
   Campus brightness means: day 112.0, night 79.1
   GardensPoint night images appear exposure-compensated or over-brightened, while campus night images are genuinely darker.

4. How challenging is the strict campus query composition before any model is used?
   Exact filename matches: 33
   Marked no-match queries: 12
   PXL unmatched queries: 19
   This means the strict campus protocol is almost half hard no-match or non-1-to-1 queries.

5. Is campus place coverage balanced?
   Unique place IDs: 10
   Largest night place bucket: entrance (25 images)
   Place coverage is uneven, so some scenes are much more represented than others.
